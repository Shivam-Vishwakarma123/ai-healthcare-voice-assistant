Maternal Health and Obstetric Danger Signs (WHO / MOHFW India)

Key danger signs during pregnancy and postpartum:
Pregnancy requires continuous antenatal care. Any of the following symptoms represents an obstetric emergency requiring immediate hospital evaluation:

Major danger signs:
1. Vaginal bleeding at any stage of pregnancy: Can indicate miscarriage, ectopic pregnancy, placenta previa, or placental abruption.
2. Severe persistent headache or visual disturbances (blurriness, spots before eyes, flashing lights): Core indicators of preeclampsia, which can progress to eclamptic seizures.
3. Severe epigastric or right upper abdominal pain.
4. Sudden severe swelling of hands, face, or ankles.
5. High fever with or without chills.
6. Premature leakage of fluid from the vagina (water breaking before 37 weeks).
7. Noticeable decrease or cessation of fetal movement in the second or third trimester.
8. Uncontrolled severe vomiting preventing hydration.

Emergency response:
Pregnant individuals with any danger sign should be transported immediately to an emergency obstetric care facility or district hospital. In India, call 102 or 108 for specialized maternal transport and ambulance support.
