Mental Health Crisis and Tele-MANAS Support (MOHFW India / Tele-MANAS)

National mental health crisis response in India:
Tele-MANAS (Tele Mental Health Assistance and Networking Across States) is the Government of India's 24/7, toll-free, multilingual mental health helpline established by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MOHFW).

When to reach out for immediate mental health support:
- Overwhelming feelings of hopelessness, despair, or wanting to end one's life.
- Acute anxiety attacks, severe panic, or unmanageable psychological distress.
- Severe depressive episodes affecting sleep, appetite, and basic self-care.
- Suicidal thoughts, self-harm impulses, or emotional crises.

Contact information and helpline numbers:
- Tele-MANAS Toll-Free Numbers: 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (available 24/7 across all States and Union Territories in India).
- National Emergency Helpline: 112 for acute safety intervention.
- Kiran National Helpline (Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment): 1800-599-0019.

Immediate safety steps:
- If someone is in immediate danger of hurting themselves, do not leave them alone.
- Remove access to potentially harmful items (medications, sharp objects).
- Call Tele-MANAS (14416) or 112 immediately, or go with them to the nearest hospital emergency room.
